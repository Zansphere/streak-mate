StreakMate Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 | Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to StreakMate ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we safeguard it, and how you can exercise your rights—including deleting your account and data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide habit tracking, streak analytics, reminders, and community features, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: When you register for StreakMate, we collect your username, email address, and an encrypted password hash.
  • Habit & Streak Data: Habits you create, goal categories (e.g., fitness, study, spiritual), habit logs, completion timestamps, streaks, and freeze days used.
  • Social & Community Data: Friend lists, friend requests, and leaderboard streak rankings if you connect with other users.
  • Device & Notification Data: Your device timezone (to schedule reminders according to your local time) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens (to deliver push notifications).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To initialize and manage your StreakMate user account.
  • To accurately calculate daily streaks, habit completions, achievements, and statistics.
  • To send scheduled habit reminder notifications and streak alert messages.
  • To enable social features such as searching friends and viewing streak leaderboards.
  • To ensure server security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical errors.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers. We only share information with reputable service providers necessary for app operation:

  • Google Firebase (FCM): Used exclusively to deliver push notifications to your mobile device.
  • Cloud Database Hosting (MongoDB Atlas): Secure cloud infrastructure where user accounts and habit logs are securely stored.

4. Data Security & Storage

We take the security of your data seriously and apply industry-standard protections:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the StreakMate mobile application and our backend servers is transmitted over HTTPS using Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL).
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed with salted algorithms (bcrypt) prior to database storage.
  • Restricted Access: Backend services and databases are secured with strict access tokens and firewall rules.

Google Play Compliance Notice: StreakMate complies with Google Play's User Data policy and Account Deletion requirements.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data at all times.

How to Delete Your Account and Data:

  • In-App Deletion (Immediate):
    Open the StreakMate app → Go to Profile → Tap Settings (gear icon) → Scroll down to DANGER ZONE → Select "Delete account" → Confirm. Your profile, habits, logs, streaks, and friend connections will be immediately and irreversibly deleted from our live databases.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request:
    If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request full account and data deletion by sending an email from your registered email address to support@streakmate.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 14 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

StreakMate is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
